# Common AI Support Questions

The questions users ask most often about AI tools, with answers the service desk and AI champions can use.

Features and settings differ between AI tools. Where an answer depends on the tool, check the supplier's documentation and your organisation's own guidance.

---

## Understanding how AI behaves

### Why does AI sometimes give different answers?

AI responses vary based on prompt wording, context, and available information.

AI tools generate each answer afresh instead of looking one up, so the same question can produce different wording, and sometimes a different conclusion. For a more consistent result, give more context, be specific about what you want, and ask for a fixed format.

### Why did it give me an answer that was wrong?

AI tools produce text that is likely to fit the question, which is not the same as text that is true. They can state incorrect facts with complete confidence, and can invent figures, quotations and references.

Treat every answer as a draft to be checked. See the Accuracy section of [AI Best Practices](AIBestPractices.md).

### Why does it not know about recent events?

Most AI models are trained on information up to a certain date and do not know what happened afterwards, unless the tool can search the web or has been connected to current data. If the answer depends on recent information, check whether your tool has that ability, and verify the answer either way.

### Does it remember what I told it before?

Within a single conversation, the tool uses your earlier messages. Whether it remembers anything between conversations depends on the tool and its settings. If it seems to have forgotten something, include the important background again.

## Getting better results

### How can I improve output quality?

- Provide more context
- Be specific
- Include examples
- Define the desired format

Also say who the audience is, and ask the tool to improve its first attempt instead of starting again. See [Prompt Engineering Basics](PromptEngineeringBasics.md).

### The answer is too long, too short or in the wrong style. What can I do?

Tell it. Follow up with an instruction such as "make this half the length", "use plain English for a non-technical reader" or "put this in a table". You can also give the length, tone and format in your first prompt.

### Why did it refuse my request?

AI tools decline some requests, usually because of the supplier's safety rules or settings applied by your organisation. Try describing the business purpose more clearly. If a legitimate work task is still refused, report it so that the guidance can be reviewed.

## Safety and data

### Should I trust every AI response?

No. AI outputs should always be reviewed and validated before use.

You remain responsible for anything you send or act on, however it was produced.

### Can I enter customer or company information?

Only if the tool is approved by your organisation for that type of information. If you are not sure, do not enter it, and ask first. See the Security section of [AI Best Practices](AIBestPractices.md).

### Is what I type used to train the AI?

It depends on the tool and the agreement your organisation has with the supplier. Business versions of AI tools often have different data terms from free or personal versions. Use the approved work version, and ask the service desk if you need to know the terms for a particular tool.

### Do I have to say when I have used AI?

Follow your organisation's policy. As a general rule, tell colleagues when AI has produced a draft or an analysis, so that they know to review it carefully.

## Getting help

### The AI tool is not working. What should I try?

1. Check whether there is a known issue or outage
2. Refresh the page or restart the application
3. Sign out and sign back in
4. Confirm that you are signed in with your work account and that you have a licence
5. Try a different browser or device
6. If it still does not work, log a ticket with the exact error message, a screenshot, the time, and what you were trying to do

### How do I get access to an AI tool?

Raise a request through the service desk or service catalogue. Use only tools that have been approved, even if others are freely available online.

### How do I report an answer that is wrong, offensive or worrying?

Use the feedback option inside the tool if there is one, and tell the service desk, including the prompt you used and the answer you received. Reports like this help to improve the guidance for everyone.

---

## Notes for the service desk

- Log AI questions under a consistent category, so that recurring themes can be seen
- Ask for the prompt and the output when investigating a problem, with any sensitive details removed
- Separate the three common causes: the tool is unavailable, the user needs guidance, or the output is poor
- Pass repeated questions back to the adoption lead, because they show where training or guidance is missing
